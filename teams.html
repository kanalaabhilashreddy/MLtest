TEAM & HIGH-PERFORMANCE ENVIRONMENTS

Teams

Support teams with psychological skills, communication and competition preparation that match the demands of the sport and the stage of the season.

Start an EnquirySee the Approach
03

Performance is individual and collective.

Team performance depends on more than individual mental skills. Roles, communication, trust, response to mistakes and the environment around competition all matter.

WHAT THE WORK CAN INCLUDE

Team work can target performance and environment.

Programmes can be designed as workshops, repeated sessions, competition preparation or a combination of team and individual support.

01

Team mental skills

Build shared language around focus, pressure, errors, communication and preparation.

  • Team routines
  • Pressure response
  • Reset strategies
  • Shared cues
02

Competition preparation

Prepare athletes for the psychological demands of an upcoming event, series or season.

  • Scenario planning
  • Visualisation
  • Role clarity
  • Pre-match routines
03

Performance environment

Work with coaches and support staff on the behaviours surrounding athlete performance.

  • Communication
  • Feedback
  • Athlete management
  • Culture

THE PROCESS

A structured process, adapted to the situation.

01

Context

Understand the sport, team and competition demands.

02

Prioritise

Identify the few psychological themes that matter most.

03

Practise

Train skills through realistic team activities.

04

Transfer

Connect sessions to training and competition.

05

Review

Use feedback to adapt the next intervention.
