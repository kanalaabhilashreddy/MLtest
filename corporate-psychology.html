THE APPROACH

Useful, contextual and performance-focused.

01

Start with the context

Understand the people, role, team and performance environment before choosing the intervention.

02

Keep it practical

Translate psychological concepts into behaviours, routines and tools people can actually use at work.

03

Build for transfer

Connect workshops and learning to the situations where people need the skill to show up.

04

Review and adapt

Use feedback and observations to refine the work rather than treating one session as the finish line.
