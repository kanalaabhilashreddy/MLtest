ONE-TO-ONE MENTAL TRAINING

Individual Performance

Structured sport-specific mental training for athletes who want to understand their performance patterns and build skills they can use in training and competition.

Start an EnquirySee the Approach
01

The athlete is not the problem to fix.

The starting point is the performance situation: what happens, when it happens, what the athlete notices, and what can be trained.

WHAT THE WORK CAN INCLUDE

From performance concern to practical skill.

Individual work is shaped around the athlete's sport, age, role, goals and current environment. The programme can involve the athlete alone or, where appropriate, coordinated support with parents and coaches.

01

Performance assessment

Understand the athlete's context, goals, strengths, challenges and performance patterns.

  • Sport and role
  • Current performance concern
  • Existing routines and coping strategies
02

Mental skills training

Build practical skills around the needs identified in the assessment.

  • Focus and attention
  • Confidence and self-belief
  • Pressure regulation
  • Emotional control
03

Competition preparation

Translate skills into a plan that can be rehearsed before competition.

  • Pre-performance routine
  • Visualisation
  • Reset cues
  • Competition plan

THE PROCESS

A structured process, adapted to the situation.

01

Understand

Map the performance context and establish the working goals.

02

Train

Introduce and practise relevant psychological skills.

03

Apply

Take skills into sport-specific training.

04

Compete

Use the plan during competition and pressure situations.

05

Review

Reflect, adjust and build the next cycle.
