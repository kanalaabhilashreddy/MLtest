ACADEMY MENTAL TRAINING

Academies

Build mental training into athlete development instead of treating psychology as an occasional add-on.

Start an EnquirySee the Approach
02

Make the environment part of the intervention.

A strong academy system gives athletes repeated opportunities to learn, practise and discuss psychological skills in the same environment where they develop technically.

WHAT THE WORK CAN INCLUDE

An academy programme can operate at three levels.

Mind Lead can work with athletes, coaches and parents so the language and expectations around performance are more aligned across the environment.

01

Athlete development

Age- and sport-appropriate group mental training that develops psychological skills progressively.

  • Focus and routines
  • Confidence
  • Pressure and errors
  • Life skills
02

Coach education

Help coaches recognise performance behaviours and communicate in ways that support development.

  • Communication
  • Feedback
  • Motivation
  • Competition support
03

Parent education

Give parents practical ways to support athletes without increasing unnecessary performance pressure.

  • Process-focused support
  • Competition behaviour
  • Communication
  • Healthy expectations

THE PROCESS

A structured process, adapted to the situation.

01

Map

Understand the academy's sports, age groups and current needs.

02

Design

Build a programme around the development pathway.

03

Deliver

Run regular sessions and practical activities.

04

Integrate

Connect mental training with coaches and sporting practice.

05

Review

Use feedback and programme observations to refine delivery.
