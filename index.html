THE SPORT MATTERS

The sport changes the problem.

A reset between points is different from a reset after a delivery, shot or sprint. Mental training has to make sense inside the sport — not just outside it.

THE MIND LEAD APPROACH

Understand it. Train it. Use it under pressure.

The skill has to survive contact with the real sporting environment.

01

Assess

Understand the athlete, sport and performance environment.

02

Identify

Find the pattern that actually needs attention.

03

Train

Build practical psychological skills.

04

Apply

Take the skill into sport-specific practice.

05

Review

Use performance information to understand what changed.

06

Adapt

Refine the plan over repeated cycles.

The objective isn't to eliminate pressure. It's to make performance more reliable when pressure arrives.
