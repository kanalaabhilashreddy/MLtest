01 / ABOUT MIND LEAD

Building a champion mind for better performance, wellbeing and enjoyment.

Mind Lead is a sport and performance psychology consultancy using current research and scientifically supported methods, translated into simple, accessible and innovative practice.

We take a holistic approach to performance — working with thoughts, beliefs, behaviours, emotions and cognitive-perceptual skills so athletes can perform better while developing as people.

Our work is practical, engaging and tailored to the individual, team or coach in front of us.
